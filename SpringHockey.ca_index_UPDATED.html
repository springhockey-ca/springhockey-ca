Spring Hockey, explained

Spring Hockey is competitive ice hockey played from mid-March through June, picking up once spring break and the winter season have wrapped up. Unlike winter minor hockey, it's privately run. Teams, tryouts, and tournaments are organized independently, and players are chosen on ability rather than registration order.

Most programs hold tryouts in the late fall or early in the new year and set their own fees. A season usually runs $600–1,200. Players are grouped by birth year, and Spring Hockey can provide additional ice time, development, tougher competition, and the opportunity to meet players from outside a normal winter association.
